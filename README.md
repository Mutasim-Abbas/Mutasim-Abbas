## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/WUlplcMpOCEmTGBtBW/giphy.gif" width="28" /> About me

I'm a software engineering student at Istanbul Atlas University, and I build for the web.

I learn by building complete things rather than following tutorials. **Every project below is
deployed and usable — not a half-finished demo.** I'd rather ship one app that really works than
ten that only run on my laptop.

Most of my work sits where AI meets web development. I like using AI as a feature inside a
product rather than as the product itself, so in my apps every AI answer is something you can
edit or ignore, and everything still works with the AI turned off.

I work mainly in TypeScript and React.

### 🥗 Modus — nutrition tracker

Offline-first, with a **real** calculation engine (Mifflin–St Jeor), a 184-food database, and meal

with a genuine right-to-left layout.

`948 unit tests` `70 e2e tests` `3 security passes`

Six sorting algorithms shown **three ways**: a 3D gantry crane that physically lifts and places
numbered boxes, an array view split into the regions each algorithm actually maintains, and a

one comparison at a time — forwards *or* backwards.

`pseudocode in lock-step` `quizzes from real runs`

### 📚 LuminaStudy — AI study platform

Drop in lecture notes or a PDF and it produces flashcards, a practice quiz, a summary and ranked
highlights — then schedules your reviews with **SM-2 spaced repetition** so you study what you're
about to forget. PDFs are parsed in the browser; the file never leaves your device. Plus a grade
calculator that answers *"what do I need on the final?"* and a multi-semester degree planner.

`mastery derived from real intervals — never hand-set`
